# From an analytical alert to a defensible reviewer handoff

Run `python -m governance.review_quality` and inspect
[`results/review_quality.json`](../results/review_quality.json).

The training draft uses an existing synthetic layering alert and its actual
reason-code evidence. It separates observations, a plausible lawful explanation,
the evidence that could distinguish the explanations, unresolved requests,
analysis and the recommended next step. Evidence references must resolve to the
same case or its counterparty links. Planted evaluation labels are prohibited.

The quality gate rejects missing observations, unknown/cross-case references,
unresolved closure proposals, missing alternative explanations and assertions of
approval or filing. Passing means structurally ready for human review. It does
not assess the truth of free-text analysis, determine suspicion or authorize
closure, EDD or reporting. The packet records zero independent reviews, zero
dispositions and zero filings.

For an actual independent review, ask a qualified practitioner to reproduce one
evidence link, challenge the lawful explanation, identify missing evidence and
critique the proposed next step. Retain the original draft, reviewer comments,
revision and explicit permission before publishing attributable feedback. This
review remains pending. No outreach or professional endorsement is implied.
